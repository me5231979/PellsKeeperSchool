How we train

Every part of the position

Keepers are grouped by skill level, 5–6 per group, so every keeper gets real reps and real coaching. No standing in line.

  • Shot stopping

    Set position, handling, and catching under pressure.

  • Diving

    Safe, powerful technique from the ground up.

  • 1v1s

    Closing down, timing, and winning breakaways.

  • Crosses

    Reading flight, starting position, and claiming high balls.

  • Distribution

    Goal kicks, punts, throws, and playing out of the back.

  • Game sense

    Positioning, communication, and organizing a back line.

Questions

Before you register

Does my child need goalkeeper experience?

Not for most camps. We group keepers by skill level, so beginners and experienced keepers each train at the right pace. Boot Camp and the HS Pre-Season Prep Camp are for experienced keepers only.

How big are the groups?

5–6 keepers per coach-led group, sorted by skill level.

What should my keeper bring?

Goalkeeper gloves, cleats, shin guards, a full water bottle, and sunscreen. For full-day camps, pack a bagged lunch.

How do I register for summer camps?

Summer registration runs through our partner, Coerver. Links go live here when dates are posted.
